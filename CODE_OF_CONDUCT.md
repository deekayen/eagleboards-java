# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project supports Eagle Scout Boards of Review. Everyone involved — whether
contributing code, filing issues, or reviewing changes — is expected to make
participation a harassment-free experience for all, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the volunteers and Scouts who use this software
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Youth protection

This project is used in a Scouting context and its data describes minors.

- Follow Scouting America's Youth Protection policies in any project space.
- Never post participant data — names, contact details, dates of birth, unit
  affiliations — in an issue, pull request, screenshot, or log excerpt. Use
  synthetic data in every example. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take appropriate
and fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior. They may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior by contacting the
maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will
result in a response appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers are obligated
to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
