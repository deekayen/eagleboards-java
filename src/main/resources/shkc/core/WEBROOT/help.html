Starting the scheduler

java -jar EagleBoardScheduler.jar -p prereg_file -a adult_history_file
  • Pre-registration auto-fill: start with -p file to load the pre-registration (auto-fill) file.
  • Adult history auto-fill: start with -a file to load the adult history (auto-fill) file.
  • Data directory: -d dir names the directory holding the data files. By default it is a directory named for today's date, yyyy-MM-dd.

Pages

  • /: the check-in page for the tablets at the door
  • /youth_register and /adult_register: youth and adult sign-in
  • /scheduler: the Event page, where boards are built and run
  • /admin: the Admin tables, every record as an editable table
  • /configure: Settings

The Event page's footer shows the address the tablets use, and QR code shows it large enough for a tablet's camera. The Event page and the Admin tables update the moment something changes; there is nothing to refresh. The check-in page shows who has signed in each time it opens.

Rooms

  1. On the Event page, press Add room, or use the Rooms tab on the Admin tables.
  2. Rooms are for final boards or for project reviews.
  3. To hold several project reviews in one room, add a room entry for each, such as 200A and 200B, both for project reviews.
  4. Move moves the selected room's board to another room, or swaps two boards. Its members go with it.
  5. Rename renames the selected room. A board in it carries on under the new name. Right-click a room to switch it between final boards and project reviews; a board already in it is not disturbed.

Status

  • Waiting (stored as Registered): signed in and ready to be seated.
  • Seated: the adults are in the room with the application, references and project workbook. The youth is still outside.
  • In review (stored as InProgress): the youth is in the board room.
  • Completed: the board is over and its decision is recorded as the result.
  • Postponed: the youth arrived unprepared and was sent away without seeing a board. This is decided before a board is seated; once the board has met the youth, its decision is the result.

A board, step by step

  1. Sign-in. Youth and adults sign in on the check-in page.
  2. Paperwork check. As youth sign in, check their write-ups and paperwork. This happens off screen. If the paperwork is not in order, select the youth and press Postpone; they can come back another month.
  3. Seating. Once the paperwork is in order:
    1. Select the youth in the Youth list. It holds everyone who has signed in, in three groups: Waiting, On a board, then Finished. Their board is built in the details pane on the right.
    2. The scheduler proposes a board: a room, a chair and the right number of members. It picks with the whole waiting line in mind: it keeps adults who can chair free for the boards still to come, and uses adults whose troop rules them out for youth still waiting. If there aren't enough adults to do that it still proposes the best board it can.
    3. Under Board members, the rules are checked as you go: too few or too many members, nobody who may chair, someone from the youth's own unit, or no free room. Beside an adult's name, the Wood Badge mark (a banded pentagon) shows someone counting the event toward a Wood Badge ticket item, and a warning triangle someone from the youth's own unit.
    4. Until you change it, the proposed board keeps up with the event: when an adult signs in, goes home or leaves a board, it is proposed again.
    5. To choose someone yourself, remove who you don't want (×) and press Add beside an adult under Add members. Fill the rest then completes the board around the people you chose; Start over proposes a whole new board. Tick Show everyone to see adults on another board, gone home, or not serving on this kind of board.
    6. The chair is the member with Chair marked. Only adults who may chair this kind of board can be marked.
    7. To use a different room, choose it under Room, or click a free room card.
    8. Press Seat board (or Ctrl+Enter). The youth is now Seated.
  4. Starting the review. When the members have finished reading:
    1. Select the youth, or click their room card.
    2. Under Leaders and parents, the details pane lists the adult who introduces the youth to the board (usually their Scoutmaster, or a leader standing in) and where they are. Locate also looks for their leaders and parents by name and unit, and Link an adult records someone who didn't say so at sign-in.
    3. Press Start review (or Ctrl+Enter). For a board of review it names whom to fetch to introduce the youth, and where they are: the adult linked to them, or else their leader. A project review has no introduction.
    4. Fetch the youth, and whoever introduces them, then press Start review in the confirmation.
  5. Someone has to leave a board, or the chair changes hands: select the youth and press Change members. Remove and add people the same way as when seating, then Save members. The room timer keeps running.
  6. Completing. When the board has decided:
    1. Select the youth, or click their room card, and press Complete (or Ctrl+Enter).
    2. Enter the board's decision and any notes: Approved; Adjourned (the board postpones its decision); or Not approved.
    3. The message that follows names the youth's leaders and parents who have signed in, so they can be fetched. The room and its members are free again.
  7. Undo. Every step, member change, room move, and Link or Disable can be taken back with the Undo button in the message that follows it, or Ctrl+Z. Reset and Postpone ask first instead.
  8. Correcting a result. On the Admin tables' Boards tab, change the Result. If the result was recorded against the wrong youth, give the youth who was actually reviewed the status Completed, the Result, Chair and Members, and set the other youth's status back to Registered with the Result, Chair and Members cleared. They can then be seated for their own board.

Room timers

Each room card shows how long its board has been in its current step, with a stopwatch, and the clock restarts at each step. While a board is convening (Seated), the timer turns overdue once the members have kept the youth waiting too long. Once the review starts, it shows running long and then overdue at the times set for final boards or project reviews. Running long is a timer clock on an orange tint; overdue is an alarm clock on a solid pink-red fill, so the two differ in shape and in lightness, not only in color. A tooltip says what each means. They are a prompt to check on the room, not a limit: nothing stops a board that needs longer. Set the times on the Settings page.

Find a person

To find which room someone is in, a youth or an adult, type part of their name in Find a person under the Rooms heading (Ctrl+F goes there). The room cards narrow to the rooms holding a match, or a room by that name, and a line below the box says where anyone found in no room is: is waiting, isn't on a board, has gone home, has finished or was postponed. Enter opens the first room found, or the youth; Esc clears it.

Admin tables

The Admin tables hold every record as an editable table:

  • Boards: every youth's board and result; correct a result here.
  • Youth: the youth signed in, and their details.
  • Youth scheduled: the youth who signed up ahead of time.
  • Adults: the adults signed in. Promote someone to chair here; their name, unit, contact and roles change in the adult history too. Add adult… signs in someone who would rather not use the tablet: search the adult history to fill the form in for someone who has served before.
  • Adult history CSV: every adult who has ever signed in, read-only. Last event is when they last signed in. Sign in for today puts the selected adult on today's list.
  • Rooms: add and remove rooms.
  • Approved proposals: every project proposal approved at an earlier event in this data folder, however long ago, for a youth who comes to their board without the signed page: who, their unit, when, the chair and the other members. Read only; the line above it says which events were read.

A youth's Status is read-only in the tables. It changes only through the Event page's steps (Seat board, Start review, Complete, Postpone, Reset and Undo), which take and free a room and its members. A result recorded against the wrong youth is taken back with Undo on the Event page, straight away.

Settings
