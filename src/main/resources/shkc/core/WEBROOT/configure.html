Where these defaults come from

Scouting America Guide to Advancement, Eagle Scout boards of review (8.0.3.0).

Convene Red — 30
“Members should convene at least 30 minutes before the scheduled board of review” to go over the application, references and service project workbook (#8). Used here as a cap: past this the candidate is being kept waiting, so the card turns red. There is no yellow stage, because the window is a limit rather than a target.
Final Yellow / Red — 30 and 45
“Eagle boards generally last 30 minutes or somewhat longer … but rarely should one last longer than 45 minutes” (#9). Counted from Start Review, when the youth is brought in — not from when the board was seated.
Project Yellow / Red — 25 and 40
District practice. A project proposal review is not a board of review, so the Guide sets no length for it.

These are guidance, not hard limits — the colors are a prompt to the room, and nothing stops a board that needs longer. Adjust them to suit how your district actually runs.
